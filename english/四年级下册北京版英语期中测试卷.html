📝 四年级下册北京版英语期中测试卷

考试时间：60分钟 | 满分：100分 | 范围：Unit 1-Unit 4

第一部分 听力（共20分）

I. 听录音，选出你所听到的单词。（每题2分，共10分）
1. A. Chinese B. maths C. English
2. A. sing B. dance C. draw
3. A. doctor B. nurse C. teacher
4. A. spring B. summer C. winter
5. A. Monday B. Tuesday C. Friday

听力原文及答案：
录音原文：1. English 2. sing 3. nurse 4. spring 5. Tuesday
答案：1. C 2. A 3. B 4. A 5. B

II. 听录音，判断句子与所听内容是(T)否(F)相符。（每题2分，共10分）
1. I have maths on Monday. ( )
2. My sister can dance very well. ( )
3. My father is a driver. ( )
4. My favourite season is summer. ( )
5. It's sunny and warm today. ( )

听力原文及答案：
录音原文：
1. I have Chinese on Monday.
2. My sister can dance very well.
3. My father is a doctor.
4. My favourite season is summer.
5. It's cloudy and cool today.
答案：1. F 2. T 3. F 4. T 5. F

第二部分 笔试（共80分）

III. 选出每组单词中不同类的一项。（每题2分，共10分）
1. A. Chinese B. subject C. maths ( )
2. A. sing B. dance C. song ( )
3. A. doctor B. father C. nurse ( )
4. A. spring B. season C. winter ( )
5. A. Monday B. week C. Friday ( )

答案：1. B 2. C 3. B 4. B 5. B
解析：
1. AC是具体学科，B是学科总称
2. AB是动词，C是名词
3. AC是职业，B是家庭成员
4. AC是具体季节，B是季节总称
5. AC是具体星期，B是星期总称

IV. 按要求写出下列单词的正确形式。（每题2分，共10分）
1. can not（缩写形式） ________
2. go（第三人称单数形式） ________
3. man（复数形式） ________
4. child（复数形式） ________
5. she（形容词性物主代词） ________

答案：1. can't 2. goes 3. men 4. children 5. her

V. 单项选择。（每题2分，共20分）
1. What ______ do you have on Monday?
A. subject B. subjects C. a subject
2. I have PE ______ Tuesday afternoon.
A. in B. on C. at
3. —What can you do? —I ______ play the piano.
A. can B. can't C. am
4. —Can you swim? —No, I ______.
A. can B. can't C. don't
5. —What does your mother do? —______ is a nurse.
A. He B. She C. I
6. My father ______ to work by car every day.
A. go B. goes C. going
7. —______ is your favourite season? —Spring.
A. What B. When C. Where
8. It's ______ in summer in Beijing.
A. cold B. cool C. hot
9. There ______ a book and two pens on the desk.
A. is B. are C. am
10. —______ is it today? —It's Friday.
A. What day B. What time C. What colour

答案：1. B 2. B 3. A 4. B 5. B 6. B 7. A 8. C 9. A 10. A

高频考点解析：
2题：具体某一天的上午/下午/晚上用介词on，是期中必考考点
6题：主语是第三人称单数时动词要加s/es，语法必考重点
9题：There be句型就近原则，靠近be动词的是单数就用is

VII. 阅读理解。（每题3分，共15分）

Hi, I'm Lily. I'm a student in Grade Four. Let me tell you about my school life.
I go to school from Monday to Friday. We have many subjects at school: Chinese, maths, English, music, art, PE and science. My favourite subject is English, because I like my English teacher very much. She is kind and beautiful.
After school, I have many hobbies. I can sing and dance. I can draw pictures too. But I can't swim. My favourite season is spring. It's warm and sunny. I can fly a kite with my friends in the park.
My father is a policeman. He is very busy. My mother is a teacher. She works in a primary school. She teaches Chinese. I love my family.

根据短文内容，判断正(T)误(F)。
1. Lily is a student in Grade Four. ( )
2. Lily goes to school five days a week. ( )
3. Lily's favourite subject is maths. ( )
4. Lily can swim very well. ( )
5. Lily's mother is a Chinese teacher. ( )

答案：1. T 2. T 3. F 4. F 5. T

答题技巧提示：
做阅读理解题先看题目，带着问题回到原文找答案，定位到关键词所在的句子就能找到正确答案，期中考试这类题很多都是原文原句哦~

VIII. 书面表达。（10分）
请你以"My School Life"为题，写一篇小短文介绍你的学校生活，包括你上学的时间、喜欢的科目、会做的事情等，不少于5句话。

参考范文：
My School Life

Hello, everyone! My name is Tom. I'm in Grade Four. I go to school from Monday to Friday. We have many subjects at school. My favourite subject is PE, because I like playing basketball. I can play basketball very well. After school, I often play basketball with my friends. I like my school life very much.
