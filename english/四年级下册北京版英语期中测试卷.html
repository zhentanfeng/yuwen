📝 四年级下册北京版英语期中易错点专项练习

范围：Unit 1-Unit 4 | 满分：100分 | 专为查漏补缺设计，重点考察高频易错知识点

⚠️ 期中高频易错知识点提示（先复习再做题）

1. 介词on/in/at必考易错点：具体星期几/具体某一天的上午下午用on，月份/季节用in，具体几点用at
2. 情态动词can易错点：can后面永远加动词原形，否定是can't，一般疑问句Can开头，回答Yes, ...can. / No, ...can't.
3. 第三人称单数易错点：主语是he/she/it/单个人名时，动词要加s/es，do→does, go→goes, like→likes
4. 名词复数易错点：man→men, woman→women, child→children, sheep→sheep不变
5. There be句型易错点：就近原则，靠近be动词的是单数就用is，复数就用are

笔试部分（共100分）

I. 选出每组单词中不同类的一项，重点考察词义辨析。（每题2分，共10分）
1. A. Chinese B. subject C. maths ( )
2. A. sing B. dance C. song ( )
3. A. doctor B. father C. nurse ( )
4. A. spring B. season C. winter ( )
5. A. Monday B. week C. Friday ( )

答案：1. B 2. C 3. B 4. B 5. B

易错点提示：很多孩子分不清总称和具体分类，比如subject是所有学科的总称，Chinese和maths是具体学科，这是期中考试的必考题哦

II. 用所给单词的正确形式填空，重点考察语法易错点。（每题3分，共15分）
1. I ________ (can) swim, so I'm afraid of water.
2. She ________ (go) to school by bike every day.
3. There are two ________ (man) under the tree.
4. Look! So many ________ (child) are playing in the park.
5. ________ (she) favourite colour is pink.

答案：1. can't 2. goes 3. men 4. children 5. Her

易错点提示：
2题：go的第三人称单数是加es不是加s，很多孩子容易写错哦
3题4题：man和child的复数是不规则变化，是期中考试高频考点

III. 单项选择，全部都是期中高频易错真题。（每题2分，共30分）
1. We have PE ________ Tuesday afternoon.
【介词高频易错题】
A. in B. on C. at
2. My father ________ to work by car every day.
【三单高频易错题】
A. go B. goes C. going
3. —Can you play the piano? —No, I ________.
【can用法易错题】
A. can B. can't C. don't
4. ________ your mother do? She is a nurse.
【助动词易错题】
A. What B. What does C. What do
5. There ________ a book and two pens on the desk.
【There be就近原则易错题】
A. is B. are C. am
6. —________ is it today? —It's Friday.
A. What day B. What time C. What colour
7. —________ is your favourite season? —Spring.
A. What B. When C. Where
8. It's ________ in summer in Beijing.
A. cold B. cool C. hot
9. He can ________ basketball very well.
【can后面加动词原形易错题】
A. plays B. play C. playing
10. My sister likes ________ pictures.
A. draw B. drawing C. draws
11. —How's the ________ today? —It's rainy.
A. weather B. season C. day
12. My mother is ________ teacher. She teaches English.
A. a B. an C. /
13. —What ________ do you have? —I have Chinese, maths and English.
A. subject B. subjects C. a subject
14. I can sing, ________ I can't dance.
A. and B. but C. or
15. —________ do you go to school? —By bus.
A. How B. What C. Where

答案：1.B 2.B 3.B 4.B 5.A 6.A 7.A 8.C 9.B 10.B 11.A 12.A 13.B 14.B 15.A

高频错题解析：
1题：具体某一天的下午一定要用on，不是in！这道题90%的孩子第一次做都会错
5题：There be就近原则，只看离它最近的名词，最近的是a book单数，所以用is，不管后面的复数
9题：can后面永远加动词原形，不管主语是谁，这是必考送分题，但很多孩子还是会错

IV. 按要求完成句子，重点考察句型转换易错点。（每题3分，共15分）
1. I can play football.（改为否定句）
________________________________________________
2. My father is a doctor.（对画线部分提问，问职业）
________________________________________________
3. I like spring best.（改为同义句）
________________________________________________
4. It's sunny today.（对画线部分提问，问天气，两种写法都可以）
________________________________________________
5. you, sing, can, English, songs (?)（连词成句）
________________________________________________

参考答案：
1. I can't play football.
易错点：can的否定直接加not，不要加don't
2. What does your father do? / What is your father's job?
易错点：问职业要用does不是do，因为your father是第三人称单数
3. My favourite season is spring.
4. How's the weather today? / What's the weather like today?
易错点：两种写法都对，注意不要少了like
5. Can you sing English songs?

V. 找出下列句子中的错误并改正，专门针对孩子常犯的语法错误设计。（每题3分，共15分）
例：I likes apples. 改为：I like apples.

1. He go to school at 7 every day. 改为：________________
2. I can plays the piano. 改为：________________
3. There are two woman in the room. 改为：________________
4. We have art in Wednesday morning. 改为：________________
5. My father like summer best. 改为：________________

答案：
1. He goes to school at 7 every day.
错误分析：主语he是第三人称单数，动词go要加es
2. I can play the piano.
错误分析：can后面必须加动词原形，不能加s
3. There are two women in the room.
错误分析：woman的复数是women，是不规则变化
4. We have art on Wednesday morning.
错误分析：具体某一天的上午要用on不是in
5. My father likes summer best.
错误分析：my father是第三人称单数，like要加s

VI. 阅读理解，考察知识点综合运用能力。（每题3分，共15分）

Hi, I'm Lily. I'm a student in Grade Four. Let me tell you about my school life.
I go to school from Monday to Friday. We have many subjects at school: Chinese, maths, English, music, art, PE and science. My favourite subject is English, because I like my English teacher very much. She is kind and beautiful.
After school, I have many hobbies. I can sing and dance. I can draw pictures too. But I can't swim. My favourite season is spring. It's warm and sunny. I can fly a kite with my friends in the park.
My father is a policeman. He is very busy. My mother is a teacher. She works in a primary school. She teaches Chinese. I love my family.

根据短文内容，判断正(T)误(F)。
1. Lily is a student in Grade Four. ( )
2. Lily goes to school five days a week. ( )
3. Lily's favourite subject is maths. ( )
4. Lily can swim very well. ( )
5. Lily's mother is a Chinese teacher. ( )

答案：1. T 2. T 3. F 4. F 5. T

答题技巧提示：
做阅读理解题先看题目，带着问题回到原文找答案，定位到关键词所在的句子就能找到正确答案，期中考试这类题很多都是原文原句，不要丢分哦！

做完后对照答案统计错题：

✅ 如果错的大多是介词题：重点复习in/on/at的用法区别

✅ 如果错的大多是三单题：重点复习第三人称单数动词变化规则

✅ 如果错的大多是can相关题：重点复习情态动词can的用法
